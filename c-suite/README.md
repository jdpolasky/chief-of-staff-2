# C-Suite

A chair is a focused agent with one job: a role paragraph, a scope of what it owns and doesn't, sometimes a short set of rules, and pointers to where its material lives. Each chair is two files: a `Skill` (the operating manual, thin enough for a cheap model to run) and a `Source` (its current state, a dated log, newest entry first). In real use a chair also collects a Research or Parts folder and an Archive folder; this repo ships only the two starting files so you can see the shape clean.

**To adopt a chair:** copy its folder into your vault's `Chief of Staff/C-Suite/`, open both files, and fill in every `{{placeholder}}`. Add the chair's name to the roster line in your `CoS Skill` note so the router knows it exists. That's it, the chair is live. One special case: if you adopt Physical Plant, also copy `docs/laws.md` from this repo into your `Chief of Staff/` folder, because that chair points to it as its governing law set.

**To retire a chair:** move its folder into `Chief of Staff/CoS Archive/` with the date, and remove it from the roster line in `CoS Skill`. Don't delete it. A retired chair is kept as history.

The five chairs here, Career Coach, Planner, Health, Money, and Physical Plant, are examples, not a required set. Rename them, merge them, split them, or invent entirely new ones for whatever your life actually needs. The fixed part is the four-part anatomy in [`docs/anatomy.md`](../docs/anatomy.md). This repo includes the Skill and the Source.
